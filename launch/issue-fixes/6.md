### What happens

`mintmap scan` prints the first 40 gaps and then `... N more (see --json)`. In the evaluation, 4 of the 11 credential paths that were visible "as a gap" were only in the `--json` output, at positions 75 to 231 of the gap list (CVAT: 75, 77 and 84 of 87; Label Studio: 230 and 231 of 259). Label Studio's personal access tokens were among them. A reviewer who reads only the text report never sees them.

### Where

`summarize()` in `src/mintmap/cli.py`:

```python
for g in cov["gaps"][:40]:
    lines.append(f"  gap {g['type']}: {g['file']} {g['where']} :: {g['detail']}")
if len(cov["gaps"]) > 40:
    lines.append(f"  ... {len(cov['gaps']) - 40} more (see --json)")
```

This is report formatting only. It does not touch the analysis, fingerprints or the lock.

- [ ] A test in `tests/` with a project that has more than 40 gaps (a generated URLconf with unresolved views is enough).
